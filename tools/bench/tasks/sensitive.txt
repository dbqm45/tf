A manager role

Shop managers need their own role in the permission map in app/auth/permissions.py.

Acceptance criteria:
- AC-1: a `manager` role may read and write orders and issue refunds (`orders:read`, `orders:write`,
  `refunds:issue`).
- AC-2: a manager may not manage users (`users:manage`).
- AC-3: the existing roles keep their permissions, and an unknown role still may do nothing.
- AC-4: all tests pass (`python3 run_tests.py`).
