Volume discount on orders

Shoppers who buy many items get a discount, and the cart shows it.

Acceptance criteria:
- AC-1: a new function `order_discount(lines)` in app/pricing.py returns the discount in cents for the order's lines
  ((unit price in cents, quantity) pairs): 5% of the order's total when the order has 10 to 49 items in total, 10%
  when it has 50 items or more, 0 below 10 items. The discount is rounded down to whole cents.
- AC-2: `order_total(lines)` returns the total after the discount.
- AC-3: the cart page (app/pages/cart.py) shows a line `Discount: -X.XX` before the total when a discount applies,
  and no discount line otherwise.
- AC-4: all tests pass (`python3 run_tests.py`).
